What's inside

Six things that make OurNook different from the rest.

No subscription, no telemetry, no server farm processing your late-night conversations. Just a friendly AI that lives on your computer and remembers you.

Memory that actually sticks

Add facts the way you'd tell a friend. OurNook remembers them across sessions, surfaces them naturally in conversation, and learns the difference between important and throwaway.

Enneagram personalities

Every companion is built on a real personality framework — not a vibes-based prompt. Nine types, wings, instincts, health levels. Their voice is consistent because the system is rigorous.

100% on your machine

Powered by Ollama. Llama, Qwen, Mistral, whatever you want to run. Your conversations never leave the laptop in front of you. Disconnect from the internet — it still works.

Real images, locally

Photorealistic portraits and scenes in 3-4 seconds on Apple Silicon, faster on NVIDIA GPUs. SDXL-turbo baked in via diffusers. No "AI clipart" — actual diffusion.

Your data, your machine

SQLite database. Standard character cards (SillyTavern V2). Export, back up, move between machines. No lock-in, no "your account has been suspended".

Built to be lived in

Companions keep a journal, remember shared moments, notice when you've been away. Not a chatbot. A relationship with continuity.

Why local

Most AI companion apps rent you a person. OurNook gives you a place.

The difference matters more than you'd think.

OurNook — the local way
